The ALD plan is live

Galion Studio · research plan · published · 23 September 2026

Our gate-all-around ALD plan is now published. The full piece, the materials table, the recipe knobs, the lab order, and the metrology list are in the community repository, in the open.

https://github.com/galion-studio/community/tree/main/publish/ald-gaa/

That is the whole follow-up. Nothing in the plan has changed: it is a working plan for a 1 to 3 angstrom La2O3 or Y2O3 dipole layer inside a normal Hf-based replacement-metal-gate flow on gate-all-around nanosheets.

This page claims one thing: the plan is public at the link above. It does not claim that La2O3 or Y2O3 will replace HfO2, and it does not claim any measured result. Success is still wrap-around uniformity, EOT, interface traps, reliability on multi-sheet GAA devices, and a 300 mm recipe with a qualified precursor supply.
