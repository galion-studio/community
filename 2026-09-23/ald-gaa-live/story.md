# The ALD plan is live

Galion Studio · follow-up · published 23 September 2026

The gate-all-around ALD plan is public. The full piece — the materials table, the recipe knobs, the lab order, the metrology list, and the caveat — is in the community repository.

https://github.com/galion-studio/community/tree/main/publish/ald-gaa/

Nothing in the plan has changed. It remains a working plan for a 1 to 3 angstrom La2O3 or Y2O3 dipole layer inside a normal Hf-based replacement-metal-gate flow on gate-all-around nanosheets: conformal ALD inside the replacement-gate module after channel release, Al2O3 cap over the hygroscopic La2O3, water purges long, anneal at or below 600 C, thickness variation under plus or minus 2 percent on 300 mm.

This page claims one thing: the plan is public at that link. It does not claim that La2O3 or Y2O3 will replace HfO2, and it does not claim any measured result. Success is wrap-around uniformity, EOT, interface traps, reliability on multi-sheet GAA devices, and a 300 mm recipe with a qualified precursor supply.
